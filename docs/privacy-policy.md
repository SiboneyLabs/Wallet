# Wallet Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 19, 2026**

SiboneyLabs ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This policy describes how the Wallet app handles information when you use it. Wallet is a general-purpose budgeting app and is not directed to children under 13.

## Who is responsible for your information

The controller responsible for information processed by SiboneyLabs is SiboneyLabs, based in Latvia. For privacy questions or requests, contact [support.siboneylabs@gmail.com](mailto:support.siboneylabs@gmail.com).

## Information handled by Wallet

### Information stored on your device

Wallet stores the balance, budget categories and amounts, budget cycle, budget history, funds-added history, and app-lock preferences that you enter or configure. This information is stored in the app's private storage on your device. Wallet has no user accounts or developer-operated server, and the app does not send these budget or balance entries to SiboneyLabs or include them in analytics or advertising requests.

This information remains on your device until you clear Wallet's app data or uninstall the app. Wallet is configured not to use Android's app backup. Biometric or screen-lock credentials are handled by Android; Wallet does not receive or store your biometric data.

### Optional analytics

On first launch, Wallet asks whether you want to allow Firebase Analytics. Analytics collection is off unless you choose **Allow analytics**. If enabled, Google's Firebase Analytics SDK may collect app and device information such as an app-instance identifier, app version, device and operating-system details, and app usage events (for example, app launches and sessions). Network information may also be used to infer a general region. Wallet does not request precise location permission.

Wallet does not send your balance, budget amounts, category names, or budget history through custom analytics events. Analytics is used to understand general app usage and improve the app. You can turn analytics off or on at any time in **Settings**. If you turn it off, future analytics collection is disabled; information already collected may remain subject to Google's retention and deletion controls.

### Advertising

Wallet shows an occasional full-screen (interstitial) ad, at most once per app session, roughly one minute after you open the app. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, Google's Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as your device's IP address, device and operating-system details, app information, ad-interaction data, and identifiers needed to serve and measure ads, in line with Google's policies. Google may use your IP address to estimate the general (approximate) location of your device, for example to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Wallet itself does not request or access your device's location.

Wallet does not request the Android advertising ID (AD_ID) permission, so ads are served without it and are expected to be non-personalized. Your balance, budget amounts, category names, and budget history are never included in ad requests.

Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), Wallet shows a consent message provided by Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting ads. Ads are requested only if the applicable consent requirements are met. You can review or change your ad choices at any time in **Settings → Ad privacy options**, which appears when this option is required in your region. You can also manage Android ad settings in your device's system settings.

## How information is used and shared

We use information stored on your device to provide Wallet's budgeting features. If you opt in, Firebase Analytics processes app and device usage information to provide usage reports and help us improve Wallet. Google LLC and its affiliates provide Firebase Analytics as a service provider and process analytics information under their terms and privacy practices. If ads are requested, Google AdMob processes the information described above to deliver and measure ads and for fraud prevention and security, as an independent controller or service provider under Google's terms. We do not sell your personal information and do not use Wallet analytics data for advertising.

Google may process analytics and advertising information in countries outside Latvia or the European Economic Area. Where applicable, Google describes the safeguards for international transfers in its privacy and service terms. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Firebase's privacy and security information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy), and [how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Legal bases and your choices

Where the GDPR applies, we process information stored on your device as needed to provide features you request. We process optional analytics, and ad-related data where consent is required, on the basis of your consent. You can withdraw analytics consent at any time using the analytics switch in **Settings**, and ad consent through **Ad privacy options** where shown; withdrawing consent does not affect processing that occurred before you turned it off.

You may also ask us about access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection rights that apply to your information by emailing [support.siboneylabs@gmail.com](mailto:support.siboneylabs@gmail.com). Because budget information is stored only on your device, you can delete it directly by clearing Wallet's app data or uninstalling the app. For analytics-related requests, include enough information for us to understand and locate the relevant app data; do not send your budget details unless needed.

If you are in the European Economic Area, you may lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority in your country. In Latvia, this is the [Data State Inspectorate](https://www.dvi.gov.lv/en).

## Data retention and security

Budget information remains in Wallet's private app storage until you clear the app data or uninstall the app. Analytics and advertising information is subject to the retention settings for the Firebase project and Google's applicable retention practices. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## Children

Wallet is not designed for or directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 or intentionally show ads to them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Wallet, contact us so we can review the request.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Wallet or its data practices change. We will publish the current version at this page and update the effective date above.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact **SiboneyLabs** at [support.siboneylabs@gmail.com](mailto:support.siboneylabs@gmail.com).
